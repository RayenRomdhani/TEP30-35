# Design: a year-by-year pathway, 2027 to 2030

Date: 2026-10-29. Author of the request: Rayen Romdhani.

## Intent

The study so far solves 2030 alone: starting from the 2024 fleet, the model builds whatever is cheapest for 2030 in one step (about 6 GW of new solar and wind in the base case). The author finds this too far from reality. Tunisia added its first 100 MW-class solar plant only in December 2025, its utility STEG is heavily indebted, and tendered capacity reaches the grid years late.

The new question stays the same ("what does one MWh saved avoid?") but is answered along a realistic path: 2027, 2028, 2029 and 2030 in sequence. Each year the model keeps everything built before it, receives the committed plants in their real commissioning year, and may add only as much new capacity as Tunisia has shown it can build in a year.

## Goals

- Four linked least-cost solves per scenario (2027 to 2030), each starting from the previous year's system.
- Committed projects switch on in their sourced commissioning year.
- Yearly build limits based on Tunisia's demonstrated pace and current tender pipeline, each a register row with its source or labelled assumption.
- Results per year and over the whole period: avoided cost, gas, CO2 and capacity per MWh saved; renewable share against the 35% target.
- Docs, figures, report and site updated to tell the pathway.

## Non-goals

- No change to PyPSA-Earth or the base network, the calibration, the weather year (2013, used for every year), the two-node network or the cost data.
- No perfect-foresight multi-period optimisation: the pathway is myopic (each year decides with that year's information), which is closer to how Tunisia actually procures.

## Decisions (from the research of 2026-10-29)

| Item | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Years | 2025 and 2026 solved once (common history, base case), then 2027, 2028, 2029, 2030 per scenario | Scenarios start in 2027; the two earlier years bring the fleet from 2024 to the start of the plan |
| Demand | 2024 demand × (1 + 3.2%)^(year − 2024), imports flat at 2024 | Register (ONEM, STEG via JICA) |
| Committed projects | `data/committed_projects.csv`, switched on in `cod_expected`; adds Voltalia Gabès 132 MW (2028). Segdoud (no date) is left to the yearly allowance | Sources in the file |
| Extra solar per year (beyond committed) | 268 MW | Tunisia's fastest year so far: solar reached 773 MW at end-2024, +53% on 2023 (Enerdata, 4 Feb 2026), i.e. 268 MW added. Typed `assumption` (derived) |
| Extra wind per year | 0 MW in 2025–2028; 75 MW in 2029 and 2030 | No new large wind farm recently; 2 GW of wind tenders cleared Jan 2026 with measurements pending; the 1.7 GW tender's wind lots were 75 MW (Enerdata, 5 Jan 2023); award to operation takes about two years (Voltalia Gabès: awarded 2026, operation 2028). Typed `assumption` |
| Batteries | 0 MW before 2029; up to 150 MW / 540 MWh from 2029 | Bazma solar-plus-storage tender, bids due 14 Oct 2026 (Mercom, Mar 2026). Typed `assumption` |
| New gas turbines | Not limited | Can be added within a year (STEG plant upgrades of 250 MW announced Oct 2026); the reserve margin needs them |
| 35% renewable target | Reported against, not enforced, in the pathway | At the demonstrated pace it is likely out of reach by 2030; forcing it would make the model either infeasible or build faster than Tunisia can |
| Savings ramp | Scenario saving reached linearly: ¼ in 2027, ½ in 2028, ¾ in 2029, full in 2030 | Programmes build up over time; ANME's own goal is a 30% demand cut by 2030 (ANME, 24 Oct 2025) |
| Shifting ramp | Same ramp for the shiftable MW | Same reasoning |
| Gas-price scenarios | Price change applies from 2027 | 2025–2026 are history |

## Outputs

- `results/pathway_summary.csv`: one row per scenario and year (same columns as `summary.csv`, plus year and renewable share against 35%).
- `results/pathway_avoided.csv`: avoided per MWh for each scenario, per year and over 2027–2030 (sum of avoided cost over sum of MWh saved).
- Figures: renewable share by year against the 35% line; capacity build-up by year; avoided cost per year.

## Constraints

- Project rules stand: sourced numbers or labelled assumptions; plain commented code; tests first; no AI attribution; no push without approval.
- The existing 2030 runs stay as they are ("unconstrained 2030") for comparison; the pathway is added beside them.

## Left to the builder

- How the year chain is wired in Snakemake (one rule per scenario-year).
- How allowances are split between the two buses (by each bus's share of existing capacity of that technology, falling back to an even split).
